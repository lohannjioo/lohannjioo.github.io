4. How we use information

We may use personal information to:

  • respond to your enquiry and verify that you are a genuine wholesale or retail prospect;
  • provide catalogues, quotations, samples, product specifications, and customer service;
  • discuss orders, private label projects, distribution, logistics, and commercial terms;
  • follow up about the products or services you requested;
  • send marketing communications where permitted by law and consistent with your choices;
  • maintain business records, prevent fraud or misuse, and protect our legal rights;

  • improve our advertising, enquiry handling, products, and services using aggregated or non-identifying information.

You may ask us to stop marketing communications at any time by using an unsubscribe option, replying “unsubscribe,” or contacting us at the email address below. Stopping marketing does not prevent us from sending messages needed to respond to an active enquiry or perform a contract.

6. How we share information

We may share information only as reasonably necessary with:

  • employees and authorized sales or customer-service personnel who need it to handle your enquiry;

  • manufacturing, packaging, fulfilment, or shipping partners when needed to prepare or fulfil your request;
  • government authorities or other parties when required by law or necessary to protect rights and safety; and
  • a buyer, successor, or adviser in connection with a merger, financing, restructuring, or sale of all or part of our business.

We do not sell your personal information for money. We do not allow service providers to use it for their own unrelated purposes.
